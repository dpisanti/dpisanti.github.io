<div class="home-intro">

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Space Robotics Group at SnT, University of Luxembourg. I develop learning-based computer vision for map-relative navigation of aerial and landing platforms on the Moon and Mars, where satellite navigation is absent, the terrain is often unknown, and sunlight redraws the scene with every passing hour.

I carried out my doctoral research at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory. On the LoRNA project I conceived Geo-LoFTR, a geometry-aided deep image matcher that registers onboard images to orbital maps despite severe changes in illumination, and MARTIAN, a framework that renders pose-annotated aerial imagery of Mars from HiRISE orbital data. Earlier, on the Lunar Crater Radio Telescope, I built the science forward model for an instrument designed to detect the faint hydrogen signal of the Cosmic Dark Ages.

## Research interests

- Map-relative localization and terrain relative navigation
- Image matching robust to illumination and scale changes
- Multimodal deep learning with orbital imagery and elevation data
- Synthetic data generation from orbital maps
- Hazard detection and 3D terrain reconstruction
- Deployment of deep networks on embedded processors
