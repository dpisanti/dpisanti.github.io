**Role:** Project Coordinator and proposal co-author, University of Luxembourg (2025 to 2026)

AI4SPACE-XPLORE (Advanced AI Solutions for Space Exploration) is a study funded by the European Space Agency to build a strategic roadmap for the integration of artificial intelligence in space exploration activities. It responded to ESA Invitation to Tender 1-12786, *Study for the Integration of AI in Space Exploration Activities*.

The consortium was led by the University of Luxembourg in collaboration with DLR (Germany). The proposal was submitted in July 2025, awarded EUR 200k, and the study was completed in September 2026.
