**Role:** Visiting Student Researcher, NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (Oct 2021 to Dec 2022)

The Lunar Crater Radio Telescope is a concept funded by NASA Innovative Advanced Concepts (NIAC) for a radio telescope on the far side of the Moon, built to detect the redshifted 21-cm signal of neutral hydrogen from the Cosmic Dark Ages.

## Contributions

- **Science forward model.** I developed the model that predicts what the telescope would observe, as the basis for assessing its science return.
- **Signal extraction.** I integrated a pattern recognition pipeline into a simulated lunar observation framework, retrieving the signal to within 20 mK over a one-year mission.
- **Simulated datasets.** I generated training and test sets of sky observations from the lunar far side with the full LCRT antenna model, including crater morphology and the dielectric properties of the regolith.

This work received the NOVA Award from the Mobility and Robotics Systems section of NASA JPL in 2024.
