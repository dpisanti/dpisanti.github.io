## MbL-Mars

Map-based localization pipeline for Mars rotorcraft, with trained Geo-LoFTR models. NASA-JPL GitHub, 2026.

[Code](https://github.com/nasa-jpl/mbl_mars) / [Weights](https://huggingface.co/dpisanti/mbl-mars) / [Project page](https://dpisanti.github.io/geo-loftr/)

## MARTIAN

Blender-based framework for rendering pose-annotated aerial Mars imagery from HiRISE orbital data. NASA-JPL GitHub, 2026.

[Code](https://github.com/nasa-jpl/martian) / [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29647)

## Geo-LoFTR Dataset

Synthetic Mars training and test data rendered with MARTIAN, including orthographic maps, navigation queries and training pairs under varied illumination. JPL Open Repository, 2026.

[Data](https://doi.org/10.48577/jpl.7JFBOE)

## LCRT Science Model

Radio sky simulations and code for the science forward model of the Lunar Crater Radio Telescope. JPL Open Repository, 2023.

[Code and data](https://doi.org/10.48577/jpl.D4SYJN)
